# **DIEM LLM-Powered Chatbot**

This project involves the development of a Large Language Model (LLM) powered chatbot designed to provide accurate and context-aware information regarding the Department of Information Engineering, Electrical Engineering and Applied Mathematics (DIEM) at the University of Salerno.

## **Project Overview**

The goal of the chatbot is to assist students, faculty, and external users by providing conversational answers to questions about DIEM services, academic activities, facilities, and official notices. By leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the system ensures that responses are factually grounded in the department's official data sources.
